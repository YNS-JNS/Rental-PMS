# Testing Discipline & QA

- **Core logic must be verifiable.** Critical workflows must be protected against regression.
- **Include unit tests** for new functionality ensuring a minimum of **80% test coverage**.
- **Use the Arrange → Act → Assert pattern.**
- **Tests must increase confidence,** not just coverage.
- **Services must be testable independently of transport.** No typing bypasses in tests.
